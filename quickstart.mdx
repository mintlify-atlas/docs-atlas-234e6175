Get up and running with ChaosPrep in just a few steps. This guide will walk you through signing in, setting your exam goals, and tracking your first study session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    ChaosPrep uses Google authentication powered by Firebase for secure, instant access.

    1. Visit the ChaosPrep web app
    2. Click **Sign In with Google**
    3. Select your Google account
    4. Grant permissions for authentication

    <Info>
    **First-time users**: Your account is created automatically on first sign-in. No registration forms, no email verification—just instant access.
    </Info>

    Once signed in, your data syncs automatically across all your devices. ChaosPrep works as a Progressive Web App (PWA), so you can install it on your phone or desktop for a native app experience.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your exam target date">
    Configure your exam preparation timeline to enable the countdown feature and set your focus.

    1. Click the **Settings** icon (gear icon) in the sidebar or header
    2. Select your **Exam Type**:
       - JEE Main
       - JEE Advanced
       - NEET
       - Custom (for other competitive exams)
    3. Choose your **Session** (for JEE: April or January)
    4. Set your **Target Year** (e.g., 2026)
    5. Confirm your **Target Date** (auto-calculated or custom)

    <Tip>
    Enable **Show Countdown** in settings to display your remaining days prominently on the dashboard. This creates urgency and helps you stay focused on your deadline.
    </Tip>

    Your target date appears in the sidebar countdown card, showing exactly how many days remain until your exam.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first daily task">
    Plan your day by adding tasks to the visual calendar.

    1. Go to the **Planner** view (calendar icon in navigation)
    2. Select today's date or any future date
    3. In the **Quick Add Task** form on the sidebar:
       - Choose a **Subject** (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology, or custom subjects)
       - Enter your **Task** description (e.g., "Complete 50 Rotational Motion questions")
       - For mock tests, the form expands to capture scores
    4. Click **Add to Plan**

    Your task appears in the calendar grid. Click the checkbox to mark it complete when done.

    <Info>
    **Subject customization**: Add custom subjects beyond the defaults in **Settings → Custom Subjects** to match your specific exam or study needs.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start tracking with the focus timer">
    Track actual study hours to maintain streaks and analyze your weekly trends.

    1. Switch to the **Timer** view in navigation
    2. Select a **Subject** for your session
    3. Click the **Play** button to start the timer
    4. Study without distractions—the timer runs in the background
    5. Click **Pause** to take a break or **Stop** to end the session

    <Tip>
    **Study streaks**: Timer sessions count toward your daily streak. Study for at least 1 minute each day to maintain your streak and build consistent habits.
    </Tip>

    The timer chart shows your weekly study hours by day. Use this to identify patterns and ensure balanced daily effort.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for new users

<Accordion title="Install ChaosPrep as a PWA">
  ChaosPrep is a Progressive Web App. Install it for a native app experience:

  **On desktop (Chrome/Edge):**
  1. Look for the install icon in the address bar
  2. Click **Install ChaosPrep**

  **On mobile (Android/iOS):**
  1. Open the browser menu
  2. Select **Add to Home Screen**

  Installed PWAs work offline, launch faster, and feel like native apps.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Offline mode works automatically">
  All your data syncs to the cloud, but ChaosPrep fully supports offline use:
  - View and edit tasks
  - Run the timer
  - Log errors and questions
  - Update syllabus progress

  When you reconnect, all changes sync automatically to Firebase.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Switch exam types anytime">
  If you're preparing for multiple exams or change your target, update your exam type in **Settings** without losing data. Your tasks, timer logs, and syllabus progress remain intact.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Understand the logical day system">
  ChaosPrep uses a "logical day" that starts at your configured **Day Rollover Hour** (default: 12:00 AM).

  If you study late at night (e.g., 2 AM) and your rollover is set to 4 AM, that session counts toward the *previous* calendar day. This protects your streak and accurately reflects your study schedule.

  Configure this in **Settings → Day Rollover Hour**.
</Accordion>
