## Master your daily chaos

ChaosPrep is a quiet, distraction-free tracking system designed specifically for JEE, NEET, and competitive exam aspirants. Zero AI fluff. Zero feed scrolling. Just the tools you need to hit your targets and visualize your growth.

### Built for serious aspirants

When you're preparing for competitive exams, every minute counts. ChaosPrep helps you stay accountable to your goals with purpose-built features:

- **Daily & weekly task planning** with visual calendar views
- **Focus timer** with study streak tracking and session logs
- **Mock test analytics** with performance trajectory graphs
- **Error logging system** to categorize and never repeat mistakes
- **Daily question tracker** to monitor solving consistency
- **Syllabus progress tracker** for JEE/NEET syllabi
- **Accountability squad** to keep friends in check with invite codes
- **Focus Radio** for distraction-free study music

<Note>
  ChaosPrep is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works offline and syncs seamlessly with Firebase. Install it on any device for a native app experience.
</Note>

## Zero fluff, maximum focus

ChaosPrep is deliberately minimal. No social feeds, no gamification gimmicks, no AI-generated motivational quotes. Just clean interfaces, real-time sync, and tools that help you:

- **Track what matters**: Study hours, mock scores, syllabus coverage, and error patterns
- **Stay accountable**: Daily streaks, weekly targets, and squad progress sharing
- **Visualize growth**: Charts for study trends, mock performance, and chapter-wise progress
- **Work offline**: PWA architecture means you can log sessions and tasks without internet

<Tip>
  ChaosPrep uses a logical day system with customizable rollover hours. If you study past midnight, your sessions still count toward the same study day.
</Tip>
