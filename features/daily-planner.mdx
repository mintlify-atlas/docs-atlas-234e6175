The daily planner is your command center for tracking JEE/NEET preparation. It uses a calendar grid interface to help you visualize your tasks, mock tests, and daily commitments.

## How it works

The planner displays all your tasks as cards organized by date. Each task card shows the subject, description, and completion status at a glance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the planner">
    Click the **Planner** button in the sidebar navigation. This view is your default home screen when you open ChaosPrep.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Browse your calendar">
    Use the left/right chevron buttons to change months. Click the **Today** button in the top-right to instantly jump back to the current date.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add a new task">
    In the sidebar (desktop) or bottom sheet (mobile), you'll find the "Quick Add Task" form:
    
    - Select a date using the date picker
    - Choose your subject from the grid of subject pills (Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology)
    - Enter your task description (e.g., "Solve Rotational Motion numericals")
    - Click **Add to Plan**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Complete tasks">
    Click the checkbox on any task card to mark it as done. Completed tasks are visually dimmed and show a checkmark icon.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding mock tests to your planner

Mock tests appear as special task cards with a purple "MockTest" subject badge and score fields.

To add a mock test to your daily plan:

1. Select the **MockTest** subject pill when creating a task
2. Additional score input fields will appear below the subject selector
3. Enter your scores for each section
4. Add the test description (e.g., "JEE Main Full Length Test #5")
5. Submit the task

Mock test tasks are automatically tracked in the [Analytics](/features/mock-tests) section.

## Task completion tracking

Your planner automatically tracks:

- **Daily completion rate**: Shows percentage of tasks completed each day
- **Subject distribution**: Visual color-coding by subject
- **Streak maintenance**: Completing tasks daily builds your study streak

<Note>
Completed tasks remain visible on your calendar but appear with reduced opacity. This helps you track what you've accomplished without cluttering the view.
</Note>

## Calendar navigation shortcuts

On desktop, you can use keyboard shortcuts:

- **Left/Right arrows**: Navigate between months
- **T**: Jump to today
- **N**: Quick-add a new task

## Subject customization

Click **+ Manage** next to the Subject selector to add custom subjects beyond the default Physics, Chemistry, and Maths/Biology. Custom subjects can be assigned unique colors from the color palette.

Your daily planner syncs across all devices in real-time, so you can plan on desktop and track on mobile seamlessly.
